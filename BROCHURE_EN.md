### POS & Business Management App — Everything in One Hand

Still recording sales in a notebook? Stock running out without warning? Supplier invoices scattered and payments forgotten?

**BayarYuk! is here to solve all of that.**

## What Is BayarYuk!?

BayarYuk! is a point-of-sale (POS) app and complete business management system designed specifically for businesses in Indonesia. From retail shops, cafes, salons, to wholesalers — BayarYuk! helps you run your business more organized, faster, and more profitable.

Available in **Bahasa Indonesia & English**, with a **complete in-app guide**.

## Key Features

### Fast & Easy POS

- Process sales in seconds — scan barcode, select items, pay, done
- Customer can't pay in full? Record it as a **credit sale** — outstanding balance is automatically tracked
- Receipts printed automatically via **Bluetooth printer** (80mm thermal)

### Always-On Inventory Control

- Monitor stock levels in real time from your phone — know exactly which items are running low
- Get **automatic alerts** when stock is almost out, so you never run out unexpectedly
- Manage **product variants** with ease — e.g. sizes S/M/L/XL, colors, flavors, and more
- Print your own **barcode labels** for every product

### Daily Sessions & Comprehensive Reports

- Start and end **daily cashier sessions** — know exactly how much revenue you made today
- View **revenue, profit, and loss** reports automatically
- Know your **best-selling products** and **most profitable categories**
- Payment breakdown by method (cash, transfer, card)
- **Export reports to PDF** — send directly to business owners or accountants

### Streamlined Purchasing

Manage your entire supplier purchasing workflow in one place:

**Request for Quotation** → **Purchase Order** → **Delivery Order** → **Invoice** → **Settlement** → **Purchase Return**

- No more lost or forgotten invoices
- All documents are linked — traceable from quotation request all the way to payment

### Customer Management & Loyalty Program

- Store complete customer data: name, phone, shipping address
- Customers automatically **earn points** with every purchase, redeemable for discounts
- With the **Advanced Loyalty** add-on, unlock:
  - **Tiered membership** — Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond — the more they shop, the bigger the automatic discount
  - **Subscription packages** — e.g. "10x Car Wash Package" or "30-Day VIP Discount"
- Loyal customers = more repeat orders = **higher revenue**

### Team & Access Control

- Add multiple users: cashier, manager, admin, or **custom roles** to fit your needs
- Set **per-feature permissions** — e.g. cashiers can only process sales, not view profit reports
- Know who performed which transaction and when

### Delivery Management

- Track all deliveries that need to be shipped to customers
- **Barcode verification on receipt** — confirm delivery received with a scan
- Complete delivery history saved

### Multi-Outlet _(Pro)_

- Manage **multiple outlets** from a single account
- View stock across all outlets in one place — know which outlet needs restocking

### Push Notifications _(Pro)_

- Get instant notifications when:
  - A new sale is completed
  - Stock is running low
  - Goods from a supplier have arrived
  - A payment is received
- Perfect for business owners who **aren't always at the store** — stay informed about everything that happens

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is my data safe?**
Yes. All data is stored on secure servers and also on your phone as a backup. Access is protected with login credentials and per-user permissions.

**Can I try it before paying?**
Yes. The Trial plan is available for free. You can try all basic features at no cost.

**What printers are supported?**

**What if I have multiple outlets?**
With the Pro plan, you can manage multiple outlets from one account, including a central warehouse and inter-outlet stock transfers.

**Can multiple employees use it at the same time?**
Yes. Each plan includes 2-3 users, and you can add more at Rp 50,000 per user per month.
